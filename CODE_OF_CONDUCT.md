# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full reference text defines the pledge, standards, scope, enforcement responsibilities and community impact guidelines.

We expect participants to treat one another with respect, accept constructive feedback and avoid harassment, personal attacks and disclosure of private information. The covenant applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation in response to conduct that violates these standards, with consequences proportionate to the impact.

For a private report, use the [GitHub private advisory form](https://github.com/KoangWy/ask-saigon/security/advisories/new) to reach maintainers; do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports promptly and fairly and respect the reporter's privacy.

The enforcement guidelines range from correction and warning to temporary or permanent exclusion; see the linked covenant for the complete policy and attribution.
