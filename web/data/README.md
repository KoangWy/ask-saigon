# Data pipeline

The CSV files are the editable source. Empty cells mean information has not been verified. Run `pnpm build-data` from `web/` to validate the files and regenerate `lib/data/generated/*.json`. The generated files are committed for review and are regenerated by the `predev`, `pretest`, and `prebuild` scripts so deployments use the CSVs in the same revision. Validation rejects invalid opening-hours syntax, missing required values, duplicate IDs, and coordinates outside the District 1 and District 3 bounding box.

Prices are in thousands of Vietnamese dong. The current seed is provisional; sources and checked dates appear in each row. Opening hours and prices are left blank when unknown.

## Source labels

Every row has a `nhan` column naming who supplied it:

| `nhan` | Shown as | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `chinh_thuc` | Official / Chính thức | A government page: the Department of Tourism portal (visithcmc.vn), a city or national tourism authority, a state museum or market |
| `doanh_nghiep` | Business-declared / Doanh nghiệp tự khai | The business's own website |
| `ca_nhan` | Personal experience / Trải nghiệm cá nhân | A traveller's route or report (from the app, after review) |
| `tham_khao` | Reference / Tham khảo | A third-party guide, encyclopedia or blog |

`nguon_bo_sung.csv` adds more source lines to a place or price (`id` is the place or price id), each with its own label, checked date and a short note of what the source confirms. Cards list every line, official first. When sources disagree (for example the War Remnants Museum ticket), both stay visible and the official one wins.

Approved contributions from `/dong-gop` and reports from the Report button are added at run time from the store; approved hours replace hours from a weaker label.

`lo_trinh.json` holds the seed routes posted by the team. Every stop must be a place id from `dia_diem.csv`.
